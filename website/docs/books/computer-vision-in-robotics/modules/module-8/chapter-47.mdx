## Lesson: Service Robotics and Human-Robot Interaction

### Introduction to Service Robotics and HRI

Service robotics represents one of the most rapidly growing sectors in robotics, focusing on robots that perform useful tasks for humans in various environments including homes, offices, hospitals, and public spaces. Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) is a critical component of service robotics, as these robots must interact safely, effectively, and naturally with humans. Computer vision plays a fundamental role in enabling these interactions by providing robots with the ability to perceive, recognize, and understand human behavior, emotions, and intentions.

The success of service robots depends heavily on their ability to understand and respond appropriately to human social cues, navigate complex social environments, and perform tasks that require understanding of human preferences and contexts. This requires sophisticated computer vision systems that can recognize faces, interpret gestures, understand spatial relationships, and perceive social situations in real-time.

### Social Robotics and Perception

#### Facial Recognition and Expression Analysis
Systems for recognizing and interpreting human faces and expressions:

- **Face detection**: Identifying human faces in visual scenes
- **Facial recognition**: Identifying specific individuals
- **Expression recognition**: Detecting emotions from facial expressions
- **Age and gender estimation**: Estimating demographic characteristics
- **Attention detection**: Determining where humans are looking
- **Identity verification**: Confirming identity for security applications

**Technical requirements:**
- Real-time processing for natural interaction
- Robustness to lighting and pose variations
- Privacy protection for facial data
- Cultural sensitivity for diverse populations
- Accuracy across different demographic groups

#### Gesture Recognition
Systems for interpreting human gestures and body language:

- **Hand gesture recognition**: Understanding hand-based communication
- **Body pose estimation**: Recognizing body positions and movements
- **Sign language interpretation**: Understanding sign language
- **Pointing detection**: Recognizing pointing gestures
- **Emotional gestures**: Recognizing gestures that convey emotions
- **Intention recognition**: Understanding intentions from gestures

#### Social Situation Understanding
